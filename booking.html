Dream Destinations

Explore Popular Cruise Spots

A few favorites with fun facts, highlights, and things to do.

Caribbean

Fun fact: The Caribbean is made up of more than 7,000 islands, reefs, and cays.

Do this: Snorkeling, beach hopping, rum tasting, and sunset catamaran cruises.

Alaska

Fun fact: Alaska has some of the largest glaciers in North America.

Do this: Whale watching, glacier tours, wildlife spotting, and scenic rail trips.

Mediterranean

Fun fact: Cruises here can visit multiple countries in one trip.

Do this: Historic tours, local food tastings, island exploring, and seaside shopping.
